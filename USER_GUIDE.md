## Getting Started

### Understanding User Roles

The system has different roles with different permissions:

| Role | Access Level | Typical Users |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **Super Admin** | Full system access | IT Administrator |
| **Pastor** | Ministry oversight, approvals | Senior Pastor, Associate Pastor |
| **Department Head** | Department-specific | Department leaders |
| **Finance Officer** | Financial operations | Church treasurer |
| **Staff** | Basic operations | Church secretary, assistants |
| **Member** | Self-service features | Regular church members |

### First-Time Login

**For System Users (Admin, Staff, Pastor)**:

1. You will receive an email invitation with your login credentials
2. Visit the admin portal: `https://your-church.com/admin`
3. Enter your email and temporary password
4. You'll be prompted to:
   - Change your password
   - Set up two-factor authentication (required)
5. Complete your profile information

**For Mobile App Users (Members)**:

1. Download or visit: `https://your-church.com/mobile`
2. Register with your phone number
3. Verify your phone with the OTP (One-Time Password)
4. Complete your profile
5. Wait for admin verification (you'll have limited access until verified)

## For Church Members

### 🏠 Mobile App Features

The mobile app is designed for self-service. Here's what you can do:

#### Installing the Mobile App

**On iPhone (iOS)**:
1. Open Safari browser
2. Go to `https://your-church.com/mobile`
3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" to install

**On Android**:
1. Open Chrome browser
2. Go to `https://your-church.com/mobile`
3. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top right
4. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
5. Tap "Install"

#### Registration & Login

##### Step 1: Register
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Register"
3. Enter your information:
   - Full Name
   - Phone Number
   - Email (optional but recommended)
   - Date of Birth
4. Tap "Next"
5. Enter the OTP sent to your phone
6. Create a secure password
7. Tap "Complete Registration"

##### Step 2: Verification
- Your account will have "Pending" status
- You can use basic features while waiting
- Full access granted after admin verification
- You'll receive an SMS when verified

##### Logging In
1. Open the app
2. Enter your phone/email and password
3. Tap "Login"
4. (Optional) Enable fingerprint/face ID for faster login

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### "Cannot Login - Invalid Credentials"

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure email/phone is correct
2. Check password (case-sensitive)
3. If forgotten, click "Forgot Password"
4. Check for account lockout (5 failed attempts)
5. Contact admin if issue persists

#### "QR Code Not Scanning"

**Solutions**:
1. Allow camera permission in phone settings
2. Ensure good lighting
3. Hold phone steady
4. Clean camera lens
5. Try manual check-in option
6. Update app to latest version

#### "Payment Failed"

**Solutions**:
1. Check mobile money balance
2. Ensure number is correct
3. Approve transaction on your phone quickly
4. Check network connection
5. Try again in a few minutes
6. Contact support if recurring

#### "App Won't Load / Slow Performance"

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Clear app cache:
   - Settings > Apps > Church App > Clear Cache
3. Update to latest version
4. Restart phone
5. Reinstall app (your data is on server, safe)

#### "Not Receiving Notifications"

**Solutions**:
1. Check notification settings in app
2. Check phone notification settings
3. Ensure app has notification permission
4. Check Do Not Disturb mode
5. Reinstall app if needed

#### "Profile Not Verified Yet"

This is normal for new members:
1. Admin needs to verify you
2. Usually takes 1-2 business days
3. You have limited access meanwhile
4. You'll receive SMS when verified
5. Contact church office if urgent

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. We use bank-level encryption (AES-256) for sensitive data. Your password is never stored in plain text. All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

**Q: Can I use the app without internet?**
A: Partially. You can view cached data offline, but check-in, giving, and real-time features require internet.

**Q: Is the mobile app free?**
A: Yes, the app is completely free for church members.

**Q: What if I change my phone number?**
A: Contact the church office to update your number. They'll verify your identity and update your account.

**Q: Can I have multiple family members on one account?**
A: Yes, you can link family members and check them in together.

### Mobile Giving

**Q: Are mobile payments secure?**
A: Yes. We use certified payment gateways (Paystack/Flutterwave) that comply with PCI-DSS standards. We never store your card details.

**Q: How do I get a receipt?**
A: Receipts are emailed immediately after payment. You can also view all receipts in "Giving History" in the app.

**Q: Can I cancel a payment?**
A: Once a payment is approved on your phone, it cannot be cancelled through the app. Contact your mobile money provider within their allowed timeframe.

**Q: Is there a transaction fee?**
A: Mobile money providers may charge their standard fees. The church does not charge additional fees.

**Q: Can I give anonymously?**
A: For online giving, your identity is recorded for tax purposes and transparency. Contact the finance officer for anonymous giving options.

### Check-In

**Q: What if I forget my phone?**
A: The usher can manually mark you present using the admin system.

**Q: Can I check in from home?**
A: No. QR check-in requires you to be at the church location (GPS verified).

**Q: What if the QR code expired?**
A: Service QR codes expire 30 minutes after service starts. Late arrivals should check in manually with the usher.

**Q: Can I check in other people?**
A: You can check in your registered family members. You cannot check in friends or unrelated people.

### Children's Ministry

**Q: What if I lose my security code?**
A: Contact the children's ministry check-in desk. They'll verify your identity and generate a new code.

**Q: Can someone else pick up my child?**
A: Only authorized pickup persons (listed in your profile) can pick up your child. Update authorized persons in your profile.

**Q: What if there's an incident with my child?**
A: Parents are notified immediately. Incident reports are available in your app under Children's Ministry.

### Privacy & Data

**Q: Who can see my information?**
A: Only authorized church staff with proper roles can access your data. Regular members cannot see other members' private information.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes. Contact the church office. Note that some records (giving history, attendance) may be retained for legal/tax purposes.

**Q: Do you share my data with third parties?**
A: No. We do not sell or share your personal information. Payment processors only receive necessary transaction details.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes. From your profile, you can download your attendance history, giving statements, and other personal records.

## Glossary

**Shepherd**: A church leader assigned to care for a small group (flock) of 12-15 members
**Flock**: The group of members assigned to a shepherd
**Service Unit**: A ministry team (e.g., Ushering, Choir, Technical)
**QR Code**: Quick Response code, a scannable barcode for check-in
**GPS Verification**: Using phone location to confirm physical presence
**OTP**: One-Time Password, a temporary code sent via SMS
**2FA**: Two-Factor Authentication, extra security layer
**PWA**: Progressive Web App, installable web application
**Sanctum**: Laravel's authentication system
**Dual Approval**: Requirement for two people to approve large transactions
